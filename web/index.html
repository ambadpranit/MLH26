Step-by-step: get your Gmail app password (2 minutes, one time)
  1. Open myaccount.google.com/security and sign in to the Google account you use for college mail.
  2. Under “How you sign in to Google”, open 2-Step Verification and turn it on. Skip this if it already says On.
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. (Can’t find it? Search “App passwords” in your Google Account.)
  4. Type the name ExamRadar and press Create.
  5. Google shows 16 letters like abcd efgh ijkl mnop. Copy them now: Google shows them only once.
  6. Come back here, type your full Gmail address, paste the 16 letters into App password (spaces are fine) and press Connect & fetch emails.
  7. Emails that mention exams or deadlines are ticked for you. Untick anything you don’t want read, then press Find my exams.
  8. Done? Delete the “ExamRadar” password on the same Google page and access stops immediately.

Use the app password, never your normal Google password. It only allows mail access, not the rest of your account.

Using another provider? (IMAP server)
It didn’t connect. What now?
  • “Gmail refused the login”: you used your normal password, mistyped the 16 letters, or 2-Step Verification is off. Make a fresh app password and paste it again.
  • No “App passwords” page: 2-Step Verification is off, Advanced Protection is on, or your school/company admin disabled it. Use the Paste tab instead.
  • “Could not connect”: check your internet and that a firewall or VPN is not blocking port 993.
  • Still stuck: in Gmail open Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP and make sure IMAP is enabled.
